Three variables define a crystal

To generate a crystal, the network needs three ingredients. Element identities tell it what atoms to use. Fractional coordinates say where they sit inside the cell. The lattice tells it how that cell repeats. Select an atom, and the picture connects directly to a row of numbers. Together, these variables describe the whole periodic structure.

Can we trust AI-generated materials?

AI can generate candidates and help us steer their composition and symmetry. Our tests also reveal where fast predictions and independent physics disagree, from forces to energy trends and structural response. So our answer is to build trust step by step: understand the generation, test the controls, and examine the physics. That is our exploration of AI for materials.
